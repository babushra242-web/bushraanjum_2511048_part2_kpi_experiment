Hypothesis Test Notes
-------------
Null Hypothesis (H0):
-------------
The new onboarding campaign (Treatment group) does not improve the Paid Conversion Rate compared to the existing onboarding experience (Control group).
________________________________________
Alternate Hypothesis (H1):
-----------------
The new onboarding campaign (Treatment group) does improve the Paid Conversion Rate compared to the Control group.
________________________________________
Test Type
------------------
•	One Tailed Hypothesis Test
•	Reason: The business decision is directional — leadership only cares if the new onboarding experience increases conversion, not if it decreases.
________________________________________
Significance Level
-----------------------
•	α = 0.05
•	Standard threshold for business experiments to balance risk of false positives with decision confidence.
________________________________________
Metric Tested
-------------------------
•	Paid Conversion Rate
________________________________________
Reason for Choosing This Metric
-------------------------------
•	Directly measures subscription growth.
•	Aligns with the company’s revenue model.
•	Reflects onboarding effectiveness, which is the focus of the experiment.
•	Serves as the North Star Metric for this analysis.
________________________________________
Interpretation Logic
-------------------
•	If p value < 0.05 → Reject H0 → Treatment onboarding improves conversion.
•	If p value ≥ 0.05 → Fail to Reject H0 → No evidence that Treatment improves conversion.
________________________________________
Connection to Business Decision:
-------------------------
•	Rejecting H0 provides statistical evidence that the new onboarding campaign drives higher paid conversions.
•	This supports the recommendation to launch the Treatment onboarding experience to all users.
•	Guardrail metrics (refunds, support tickets, engagement) must also be monitored to ensure growth is sustainable and customer centric.
