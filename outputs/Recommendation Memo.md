Recommendation Memo
----
Executive Summary

The new onboarding campaign was tested against the old one. The Treatment group showed better conversion, revenue, and engagement without major risks.
________________________________________
North Star Metric
----
Paid Conversion Rate
•	Control: 3.17%
•	Treatment: 6.99%
This is the main measure of success because it directly drives revenue growth.
________________________________________
KPI Tree (Simplified)
----
•	Conversion Rate (North Star) 
o	Acquisition: Landing page visits, trial starts
o	Onboarding: Completion rate, days to convert
o	Revenue: Avg revenue per user, per converted user
o	Guardrails: Refunds, support tickets, engagement
________________________________________
Experiment Results
---
•	Conversion nearly doubled in Treatment.
•	Revenue per user and engagement scores improved.
•	Refunds rose slightly but stayed low.
•	Support tickets stayed stable.
________________________________________
Hypothesis Test
----
•	Test: One tailed z test for conversion rates
•	Result: p value = 0.0008 (< 0.05)
•	Decision: Reject null → Treatment improves conversion.
________________________________________
Guardrail Metrics
----
•	Refund rate: Slight increase, acceptable.
•	Support tickets: No rise.
•	Days to convert: Faster in Treatment.
•	Engagement: Higher in Treatment.
No guardrail shows serious risk.
________________________________________
Segment Insights
----
Improvement was consistent across region, device type, traffic source, and plan type. No segment declined.
________________________________________
Final Recommendation
-----
• Launch the new onboarding campaign to all users.
•	Conversion, revenue, and engagement improved.
•	Guardrails are stable.
•	Benefits apply across segments.

Risks & Limitations
---
•	Long term churn not yet visible.

•	Refunds should be monitored after rollout.
